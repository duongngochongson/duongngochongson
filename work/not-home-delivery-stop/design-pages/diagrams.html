Branching (slide 7)

Two stops, one frame each. Boxes are decisions, arrows are choices with their points, rounded boxes are how a stop ends; a dotted arrow leads to the next stop. Long prompts are shortened.

    AI chat (slide 5)

    The learner types up to two messages to an AI customer, then a separate AI grader scores the call against the three call moves. If the AI is not available, the slide falls back to a self-check. Try again starts a new call, up to three in all.
