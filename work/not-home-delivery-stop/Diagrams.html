1Action map

The map starts from the business goal and lists what drivers must do at a not-home stop. Each action has the barriers behind it, a practice in the module and the minimum information it needs. Two barriers sit outside training, so their fixes go to operations instead of into the module.

2The path through the module

Seven slides in a row, each practising one or two of the actions in the action map. The scored slides add up to 100 points. The results screen shows the score against the pass rule, Try again reopens only the slides that lost points, and the SCORM package keeps the progress and the best score in the LMS.

3Two branching stops

Slide 7 puts the driver at two doors, an STD parcel and then a SIG parcel, with three decisions at each. The best choice earns points and leads to the next decision; the others end the stop as returned or as a violation, or go on with no points. Every ending of the first stop leads on to the second.

4The AI call on slide 5

On slide 5 the driver phones Mr. Hayes about an AGE parcel and types what they say. One AI plays Mr. Hayes; a separate AI grader, which never sees his prompt, scores the whole call against the three call moves. The slide is practice only, and it can always be finished: if the AI does not work, it switches to a self-check.
