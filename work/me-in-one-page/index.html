E-LEARNING · MOBILE MICROLEARNING

Not-Home Delivery Stop

GOAL

Train parcel drivers to complete more first-attempt deliveries when nobody answers the door.

  • Design: Training needs analysis with action mapping separated training from operations issues, then a mobile branching scenario and job aids walk drivers through the stop.
  • Result: SCORM package tested in an LMS (SCORM Cloud) that tracks learner completion, score and progress. KPIs (first-attempt success 45% → 65%, claims ≤ 0.5%) with a Kirkpatrick four-level evaluation plan.
SCENARIO-BASED E-LEARNING

Hear Them Out · Why Members Are Unhappy

GOAL

Train frontline staff to handle member complaints, raising the feedback score from 2.7 to 3.8.

  • Design: Needs analysis with the Behavior Engineering Model found three root causes, each with its own fix: training, job aids, process changes and signs. Storyboard and HEARD-model branching scenario with consequence-based feedback.
  • Result: A web-based e-learning course with voiced calls and an AI practice call, plus outcomes aligned with member survey results for evaluation.
