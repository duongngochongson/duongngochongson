Cozy in 60 seconds is a guide of eight short screens in the Cozy app, made for a phone. In about a minute, a new member learns the three zones, where to take a call, how the kitchen works and the signs they will see in the space, so they recognize the signs later. The guide shows one screen at a time and moves on when the member taps.

The guide and the signs are Cozy’s solution to two problems that members wrote about in the member survey: noise and phone calls in Focus, and the shared kitchen and fridge. Members were unsure which zone they were in, what its rules were and when the fridge was cleared. Focus and Flex looked the same, and the rules were written in the handbook that members get when they join. The signs put the rules where members work, and the guide shows them on a new member’s first day. The guide starts by asking how the member works, and the zone map then marks the zone that suits them: Focus for deep work, or Flex for calls.

The four rules behind the guide and the signs

Each sign is placed where the behavior happens, such as the Focus sign where Focus starts. Each sign and screen says what to do, and next to that what to do instead, such as “Calls and meetings in Flex”. Every sign and screen uses the same icon, word and color for each zone. Members see the rules on their first day, in this guide.

Tap through the eight screens to try the guide. Each screen stays in place until you tap to go on, so you can read at your own pace.
