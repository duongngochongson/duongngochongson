Cards 01 to 03 come from Hear Them Out, with the same words the course shows and prints: the HEARD card for the call, the Options card for what staff can offer, and the log card for following a case through.

Card 04, the Wi-Fi Down card, is a one-page checklist kept at the front desk. During an outage, staff read each step and do it before the next. All four cards share one layout, so the Wi-Fi Down card reads like the cards staff know from the course.

Why the Wi-Fi Down card works this way

These reasons are not printed on the card.

  • Job aids suit tasks that are rare, complex or costly to get wrong (Rossett and Schafer, 2007). Outages are rare and stressful, so the steps sit on a card, in the same order on every shift.
  • A wait with no known end feels longer (Maister, 1985), so the first notice goes out within 10 minutes, before the cause is known, and every message says when the next one comes.
  • Incident teams tell users early, update them at a set pace and own the problem, even when a supplier caused it (Atlassian Statuspage).
  • Everyone present gets the same compensation on every shift, because a procedure feels fair when it is applied the same way (Leventhal, 1980). The free hour gives back what members lost: working time (Roschk and Gelbrich, 2014).
