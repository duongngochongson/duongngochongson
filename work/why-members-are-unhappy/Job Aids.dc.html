Cards 01 to 03 come from Hear Them Out, word for word as the course shows and prints them. Card 04 is the Wi-Fi Down card, a one-page READ-DO checklist kept at the front desk.

Every card has the same parts: the eyebrow "Job aid · Front desk · [number]", the title, an icon, rows with a key and a bold lead, and the band "Front desk · Ask here" across the bottom.

Why each step, for the portfolio page

Not on the printed card

  • A wait of unknown length feels longer, so the first notice goes out early and every message gives the next update time (Maister, 1985).
  • Incident teams communicate early, at a set pace, and own the problem even when a provider caused it (Atlassian Statuspage).
  • One compensation rule for everyone present, on every shift, because a procedure feels fair when it is applied the same way each time (Leventhal, 1980). The free hour matches what members lost: working time (Roschk and Gelbrich, 2014).
  • Outages are rare and stressful, so the steps sit on a card at hand rather than in a course (Rossett and Schafer, 2007).
