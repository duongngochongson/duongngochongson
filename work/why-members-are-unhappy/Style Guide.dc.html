This guide sets one look for Hear Them Out and for the pieces that belong to Cozy itself: Cozy Desk, the job aid cards and Cozy in 60 seconds, the guide for new members. Every value comes from the built course, and a test checks that each stylesheet uses the tokens, the named values for color, size and speed.

The course layer is the dark desk that fills every screen, and what the course puts on it:

  • The ground is night #1F1A12, like a desk at night. Text sits on paper #FBF7EE, with ink text, 16px corners and the raise shadow.
  • The phone is dark too, and events such as "Call ended" are mono chips on the ground.
  • Son's messages are dark boxes with a 1.5px dashed outline, so his words never look like a member's.

Cozy's world is everything that carries the Cozy logo: Cozy Desk, the member card, the survey card, the job aid cards and Cozy in 60 seconds. It uses the light theme, even on the dark desk: paper, ink, desk #F4EFE4 for quiet areas, mono for the system's voice, and zone colors for wayfinding. Son and the dark ground never appear in it.

Contrast is the WCAG 2 ratio against paper #FBF7EE and the ground #1F1A12. A test checks that text reaches 4.5:1 and borders and icons 3:1, and that no stylesheet uses a gradient or a color outside the tokens.

Zones. Focus, Flex, Private and the kitchen each have a color and a light tint, for wayfinding only. A zone color always comes with the zone's icon and name, and never appears in the logo.

Feedback. Right is green and Not yet is red, each with its icon and word, in a darker shade on paper and a lighter one on the ground.

  • No text is smaller than 16px, and a test checks every stylesheet.
  • Son's lines and the learner's lines are upright, never italic.
  • Quotes and apostrophes are curly, and an ellipsis is one character.

Spacing steps run on a 4px base, from 4 to 88px. Corners grow with the size of the part, from 6px on a tag to 44px on the phone, and four shadows set how far a part sits above the desk.

  • Breakpoints. Below 760px, the call bar replaces the phone, panels become bottom sheets, and Cozy Desk stacks into one column. From 1100px, Son's bar is fixed at the top right, and the results take three columns.
  • Focus ring. A 3px lamp outline, 2px outside the control, around a 2px ink ring, so it shows on paper and on the ground.
  • Targets. Every target is at least 44px, main buttons and choices are 56px, and fields are 48px high.
  • Safe areas. Padding is at least the phone's safe area, and heights use dvh, so nothing hides under a notch or the keyboard.
  • The symbol is a rounded square with the lamp disc in its top right corner. The wordmark is "cozy" in Archivo Bold, with the lamp disc as the o. The lockup sets them side by side.
  • The disc is amber in the light and dark tones. It also marks "you are here" on a floor plan and the live dot.
  • The smallest sizes are 96px wide for the lockup, 72px for the wordmark and 16px for the symbol, with clear space as wide as the disc on every side.

One set of line drawings on a 24px grid, with a 2px stroke and rounded ends. Each icon takes the color of its text, has one meaning, and is hidden from screen readers unless it has a label.

The faces are Open Peeps by Pablo Stanley, drawn with DiceBear. Sofia has ten moods, Son four, Omar two and Dan three. A mood always shows as a word next to the face, such as "Sofia · annoyed", and a lamp ring marks Sofia's face while she speaks.

The components keep the names and props of the Cozy design system.

Document layer

The project's documents share a reading-progress bar, a section head, a callout and a stat strip, in six accent tones. Neighboring sections take different tones, and a callout's tone matches what it holds: amber for a note, blue for evidence, purple for a method, brown for background and red for a warning.

The header counts the reading time, the sections and the figures from the page itself, and dense figures open full screen with Enlarge.

Motion is calm: one thing moves at a time, and each movement finishes before the next one starts.

Morphs carry one state into the next: the lamp disc into the Cozy symbol, the face-down phone into the incoming call, a picked line into its bubble, a noted line into the note, and the desk computer's screen into the Cozy Desk window.

Reduced motion, when the device asks for it, turns slides, scales and morphs into fades in place, and the loops, such as the ring and the pulse on Accept, stand still.

  • The three cues were made for the course with ffmpeg, so none sounds like a brand.
  • Sound starts after the learner's first tap. The learner picks Sound on or Keep it quiet, and the Speaker button changes it during the call.
  • Captions stay on, and only Sofia's lines are spoken. Pause and save stops all audio.
  • Plain US English in short sentences, sentence case, no em dashes and no exclamation marks.
  • A middle dot joins a name and its detail: "Son · Your guide", "Sofia · annoyed".
  • Buttons name their action: "Save entry", "Call Sofia", "Close the case".
  • Son's lines are short and warm, and say what a phone call hides: "Looks like she feels questioned. She's giving you less."
  • Members speak in everyday words, one to three sentences to a bubble.
  • To members, Cozy is short, kind and specific, with the alternative next to the rule: "Silent zone. Calls and meetings in Flex →". To staff, it is direct and step by step: "Due today: Call Sofia back (#0418)".
  • Dates are in US order, a weekday in speech and a short date in Cozy Desk. The same thing always has the same word: member, case, owner, to-do, the note, the log.

Each job aid card has a Print button, which prints the card alone on white, centered, 140mm wide, with 12mm margins and exact colors. Every card has the same parts: the eyebrow "Job aid · Front desk · 01", the title in Archivo 700, an icon, rows with an amber key and a bold lead, and the band "Front desk · Ask here" across the bottom. The Wi-Fi Down card is wider, with a time and an "Avoid:" line on each step, and fits one page.
