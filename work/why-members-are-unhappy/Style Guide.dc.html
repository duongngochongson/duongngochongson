This guide sets one visual and interaction system for Hear Them Out, the short course for the front desk of Cozy, a fictional coworking business with three spaces, and for the pieces of Cozy's own world: Cozy Desk (the front desk software the learner logs the case in), the job aid cards (HEARD, Options and the log card), the Wi-Fi Down card (a one-page READ-DO checklist for staff) and Cozy in 60 seconds (a short, phone-first guide for new members). Every value comes from the built course: the tokens and base styles in src/design, the components in src/components and the screens' stylesheets. Lines that carry the system to the two pieces made outside the course start with "Recommendation:".

The course layer is the desk and everything the course puts on it.

  • One dark ground for every screen, like a desk at night: night #1F1A12, the top bar included.
  • Text sits on light boxes, like paper under a lamp: paper #FBF7EE, ink text, 16px corners, the raise shadow. Bubbles, decision cards, the note, the intro cards and the results all sit on paper.
  • Large headings sit on the dark ground in paper; long text there is night thought #E8DFD0, secondary text night soft #CFC4B1.
  • The phone is a dark object: a deep screen #120F0A in a night surface bezel #2A2318.
  • Son is the one exception to paper: a dark box with a 1.5px dashed outline in line strong #8C8170, his line in paper, upright.
  • Events such as "Call ended" are pill chips on the dark ground, in mono.
  • The lamp #F2A93B is the one light: the disc at the start, the focus ring, progress, Son's button, the rings, and a 4px lamp edge on a note on paper.

Cozy's world is everything that carries the Cozy logo or comes from Cozy's software and print: Cozy Desk, the member card, the case status box, the open cases list, the reminder, the survey card, the desk computer's sign-in and the job aid cards.

  • Always light (data-cz-theme="light"), even on the dark desk: paper surfaces, ink text, desk #F4EFE4 for quiet areas, line #E3DACB for dividers, mono for the system's voice, and zone colors for wayfinding.
  • Cozy Desk is a light software window on the desk: paper, 16px corners, the pop shadow, and a header with the symbol, "Cozy Desk" and "On shift: [name]" with the lamp dot.
  • Cozy's boxes in the course carry the logo at their top: the symbol on the member card and the case status box, the lockup on the survey card.
  • Recommendation: the Wi-Fi Down card and Cozy in 60 seconds belong to Cozy's world: a paper page, the Cozy logo, ink text, mono labels, zone colors with icon and name. Son, his dashed box and the dark ground stay in the course layer.
  • Recommendation: build Cozy in 60 seconds from the course's wayfinding parts: the zone map as the FloorPlan (each room in its tint and border, with its icon and name, and an amber disc for where the reader is), each sign as a ZoneBand with its alternative ("Silent zone. Calls and meetings in Flex →"), and the kitchen in its color with its icon and name. One column for screens 320 to 375px wide, with 16px side gutters.

Contrast is WCAG 2 contrast, computed with src/design/contrast.js on the build's paper #FBF7EE and ground #1F1A12. tokens.test.js checks 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for borders and icons. The build's paper is #FBF7EE, set in base.css; tokens.json and tokens.css keep #FFFCF5. Use #FBF7EE. Fills are flat: a test rejects gradients and literal colors in the stylesheets.

Layout

Focus ring. A 3px lamp outline 2px outside the control, around a 2px ink ring, so it shows on paper (the ink) and on the dark ground (the amber). A heading or card that takes focus on a new step shows no ring. Radio cards and the member search show it around the whole box.

Targets. At least 44px everywhere (--cz-target); 56px for main buttons, choices and call bar buttons (--cz-target-l); fields 48px high (--cz-control-h); Accept and Decline are 72px discs, the controls in a call 60px; date picker days 44px. Every control uses touch-action: manipulation.

  • The disc is the lamp: amber in the light and dark tones. The amber disc also marks "you are here" on a plan, the live dot and the front desk band, and it is never text on light.
  • Minimum sizes: lockup 96px wide (25mm in print), wordmark 72px wide (18mm), symbol 16px (5mm). Clear space on every side: the symbol's disc. (Brand rules from the design system handoff.)
  • In the build: the symbol at 112px as the lamp lands in it, then 56px with the wordmark beside it over the intro cards; 28px (dark) beside "Front desk training" on the Welcome; 72px (dark) as the desk computer starts and its lamp lights; 32px (28px below 760px) in the Cozy Desk header; 24px on the member card and the sign-in; 22px on the case status box; the lockup at 26 on the survey card.

One line set on a 24px grid with a 2px stroke, round caps and round joins, drawn in the current text color with no fill (a star and the Noted note fill when on). An icon is decorative unless it has a label. Sizes run from 16 to 32px, most often 18 to 22px. Each icon has one meaning.

Recommendation: a new icon for the Wi-Fi Down card or Cozy in 60 seconds (Wi-Fi, a mug, the fridge) is drawn the same way and keeps one meaning.

Faces are Open Peeps by Pablo Stanley (CC0 1.0), made with DiceBear's open-peeps style: one SVG per person and mood in public/faces/<person>-<mood>.svg, 704 × 704.

How faces are shown:

  • Round, on a disc: desk #F4EFE4 by default; paper in bubbles, in Son's boxes, in the survey comment and on After the call.
  • Sizes: 32 and 40 (Son's bar, folded and open), 36 and 44 (Son's chat), 44 (member bubbles), 48 (call bar, practice list), 56 (survey comment), 64 (member card, AI practice), 72 (the talk with Dan), 124 and 128 (the phone in a call and while it rings), 160 (After the call).
  • The lamp marks the voice: a 4px lamp ring around Sofia's face while her line plays, and a soft lamp ring 6px out that pulses while her call rings.
  • A declined or ended call dims her face to 50%.
  • The mood is a word next to the face: "Sofia · annoyed" over her bubble (the name alone while she is neutral), a mood chip under her name on the phone, the mood under her name on the call bar, "Omar · tired" in the practice. Son's name tag reads "Son · Your guide".

Cozy's world

Shared by both layers

Document layer

The written pieces of this project share four parts: a reading-progress bar at the top of the page, a section head, a callout and a stat strip. Six accent tones carry them. A tone marks what a section is for, never decoration: amber frames and sums up, blue holds evidence, green marks what works, red marks the problem, purple marks method, brown marks background and reference.

The stat strip takes a now value and a goal value on one scale, and fills both bars when it scrolls into view. Numbers that have no goal stay in a table.

Three more parts carry the reader rather than the argument. The masthead meta row counts the document itself after it loads, so the reading time, the number of sections and the number of figures are measured, never typed. Dense figures carry an Enlarge button and open full screen on a night ground, since a branch map does not fit a 760px column; Escape, the Close button or the ground closes them. Long reference lists fold into groups with their counts, closed by default.

Numbered lists carry a 30px amber circle, bulleted lists a 7px amber dot, so a sequence and a set never look alike. Subheads carry a 6px lamp bar.

Calm, never rushed: one thing at a time, each movement finishing before the next. The values live in src/motion/presets.js and base.css.

Morphs. When one thing becomes another, it morphs (1.1s unless noted):

  1. 1
    The lamp disc rises into the lamp of the Cozy symbol (2.2s, slowing as it lands); the square fades in around it (1s, from 1.3s).
  2. 2
    Over the intro cards, the symbol shrinks from 112 to 56px, and "Cozy" from "Welcome to Cozy", drawn as the wordmark, moves in beside it.
  3. 3
    The face-down phone turns over into the incoming call.
  4. 4
    On desktop, the ringing phone moves from the middle to the left as Sofia's member card slides in.
  5. 5
    The incoming call screen becomes the call; the call buttons scale in from 90%.
  6. 6
    The line the learner picks or taps becomes their bubble.
  7. 7
    A noted line flies into Your note, shrinking to 15%.
  8. 8
    At Part 2, the phone settles face down as the desk computer comes in.
  9. 9
    After sign-in, the computer's screen grows into the Cozy Desk window.
  10. 10
    The survey stars fill one by one.

Entrances and exits. Things fade in with a 14px rise and leave with a 7px rise. A bubble grows from its speaker's side (from 92% and 12px, anchored at the speaker's bottom corner); Son's messages grow from the left. Intro cards slide 40px in from the side they move from. A screen fades out before the next fades in, starting at its top. Panels, sheets, the date picker and the member list rise 8px. Son's bar fades one line out before the next. Note lines slide in 12px from the right and leave to the left.

Loops run for as long as they are needed.

Reduced motion (set on the device): MotionConfig reducedMotion="user" turns off slides, scales, turns and morphs, so things fade in place. Panels, the date picker and the member list fade in over 250ms, and hover changes are instant. The lamp, its rings, the power button, the voice bars and the computer's lamp stand still; the face-down phone shows one still ring; Accept and End show a still 4px lamp ring (3px on the call bar); Son's button keeps a still 3px glow; typing dots fade without the bounce; the starting ring turns once every 3s. A noted line stays in place while the note's count changes, and scrolling moves at once.

Scrolling is smooth. The conversation follows new lines only while the learner is within 140px of the bottom. Focus moves without scrolling, a button never makes the page jump, the top bar stays in view and panels are fixed to the window.

  • The three sounds are original, written for the course and like no brand's ringtone or chime, made with ffmpeg: mono MP3 at 32 kbps.
  • Sound plays only after the learner's tap: the first tap unlocks the two players, the voice and the cues.
  • Sound is on by default. Before your shift offers "Sound on" or "Keep it quiet"; Speaker switches it during the call; "Sound off" shows once on the phone or the call bar.
  • Captions are always on, and the top bar says so ("Captions on"): the captions icon and the caption in mono, announced politely.
  • Sofia is the only voice. Son, the learner and Omar are text only. While her line plays, her bubble shows "Playing" with five amber voice bars and her face has the lamp ring; with sound on, Replay plays it again.
  • A missing recording falls back to the reading pace and never stops the course.
  • Pause and save stops everything, the audio and the call clock included.
  • Plain, short US English. Sentence case for headings, buttons and labels.
  • No em dashes. No exclamation marks, except "Let's rock!", the Welcome's main button.
  • A middle dot joins a name and its detail: "Part 1 · The call", "Son · Your guide", "Sofia · annoyed", "Job aid · Front desk · 01".
  • Buttons say the action: "Sign in", "Call Sofia", "Save entry", "Check my entry", "Close the case", "Print", "Start over", "Keep my progress".
  • Son's button is "Next" when he has more to say, "Got it" when something waits behind his line or nothing does, or the action itself ("Show me around", "Her details", "Call her back", "Let's rock!"). His last line before the learner's own move has no button: the move comes in with it.
  • Son's voice: short, warm guidance, text only, upright. He says what a phone call hides ("Looks like she feels questioned. She's giving you less."), and on the first try he never hints at an answer before a decision.
  • The member's voice: everyday spoken words in Archivo, one sentence to a bubble ("Yesterday someone was on the phone right next to me for half an hour.").
  • Cozy to members (signs, Cozy in 60 seconds): short, kind and specific, always with the alternative: "Silent zone. Calls and meetings in Flex →".
  • Cozy to staff (Cozy Desk, job aids, the Wi-Fi Down card): direct and step by step, with mono labels: "Open cases", "On shift: [name]", "Due today: Call Sofia back (#0418)".
  • States as words: "Right", "Not yet", "Met", "(empty)", "Choose one", "Choose a date", "Tap to fill", "[n] fields need you before saving: [fields]."
  • Dates in US order. In speech, the weekday ("by Wednesday"); in Cozy Desk and the note, the short date ("Wed, Oct 7"). Times appear only in notes, in 24 hours ("10:16"). The course's days are working days: "In 1 day" and "In 2 days" count working days.
  • Terms: member, community manager, front desk, Cozy Desk, case, owner, to-do, the note, the log, job aids, and the zones Focus, Flex, Private and Kitchen.

The job aid cards (PrintableCard, JobAidCard):

  • Each card has one button, Print. It copies the card onto a sheet of its own and opens the browser's print dialog, where the learner can also save a PDF.
  • On the page: the card alone, on white, in the middle of the page both ways, 140mm wide, with 12mm page margins, no shadow, and its own colors printed exactly (print-color-adjust: exact).
  • Anatomy (10px corners and the raise shadow on screen): the eyebrow in mono ("Job aid · Front desk · 01"); the title in Archivo 700, 28 / 1.05, -0.02em; a 30px icon at the top right, in the zone's color when it is a zone; rows with a key in amber ink (Archivo 700 at 17) and the text in IBM Plex Sans 400, 16 / 1.35, with a bold lead in 600; an optional foot on desk; and the band across the bottom: ink, 40px high, the lamp dot and "FRONT DESK · ASK HERE" in mono capitals with 0.08em tracking (or a zone band).
  • The three cards: 01 HEARD card (icon desk, keys H, E, A, R, D), 02 Options card (icon focus in Focus blue, keys 1 to 4), 03 The log card (icon cases, keys 1 to 5).

The Wi-Fi Down card
