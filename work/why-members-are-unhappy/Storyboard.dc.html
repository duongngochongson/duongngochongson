How this course works

Five cards, one at a time. Each has an icon, a title and short points with the key words in bold. Under each card, ‹ and › sit on both sides of "[n] of 5": ‹ goes back a card (not on the first), and › goes on. On the last card, › becomes the round Got it tick, which leads to the Welcome.

  1. 1
    About this course (icon: info)
    • A simulation of a shift on Cozy's front desk. You play the community manager: you take a call from an upset member, then log it and follow it up.
    • Members who complain want two things: to feel heard, and a fix they can count on, on time.
    • You'll practice both: listening until the member feels heard, solving the right problem, and logging it so it gets done.
    • The goal for Cozy: members rate how we handle complaints higher.
  2. 2
    Cozy in 10 seconds (with a small floor plan of the zones and "You · on the front desk")
    • Focus is for silent work. No calls.
    • Flex is for desk work, calls and meetings.
    • Private has rooms for members on a dedicated plan.
    • You're on the front desk.
  3. 3
    Part 1 · The call (icon: phone)
    • A member calls the front desk, upset. At each moment, you pick what to say.
    • Aim for the member to feel heard, not just answered. A quick fix on its own won't land.
    • After each choice, I'll tell you how they're feeling.
    • Mistakes are part of it. You'll see how it lands, then try again from where it turned.
  4. 4

HEARD card

  • Hear: Let them tell it ("What happened? Take me through it."), and let them finish ("Go on, I'm listening.").
  • Empathize: Name the feeling ("That sounds exhausting"), and say their need back in your own words ("So you need somewhere you can rely on"), before any rule or fix.
  • Apologize: Say exactly what Cozy got wrong ("We missed your message, and that's on us"). Names stay private on both sides ("I can't share names, the same way I'd never share yours").
  • Resolve: Offer an option that fits the need. Agree on one next step with an owner and a day.
  • Diagnose: Keep the case open until the member says it worked. Log what happened, where and when; the weekly review uses the log to find the cause, not only to fix this case.

Options card

  • A Focus desk, with the zone map: fits when the member needs reliable quiet and sits outside Focus.
  • A private talk with the other member, keeping the reporter's name out: fits when the other member broke a zone rule.
  • Pausing the plan from the end of the month: fits when the member needs a break.
  • Passed on: money questions and Wi-Fi outages over 30 minutes go to the head of operations. Safety and harassment go to the incident process.
